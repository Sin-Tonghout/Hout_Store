Telegram

Order and payment notifications are sent to your configured Telegram chat.

Configuration

Set TELEGRAM_ENABLED, TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN and TELEGRAM_CHAT_ID in your .env file, then restart the server, to change this.

What gets sent

  • 🛒 New order — when a customer places an order
  • ✅ Payment success — when a payment is confirmed
  • ❌ Payment failed — when a payment attempt fails
  • ↩️ Refund — when an admin refunds an order
